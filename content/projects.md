## At home and personal experiments

These are descriptions of personal projects, with no public demo offered here.

**Public Art Map — home version** — A mobile photo feed and map seeded from Wikimedia and OpenStreetMap, focused on Atlanta. The original repository above is a different implementation.

**Google Reader 2026** — The RSS reader I missed. Add a feed, press `j` and `k` to move through it, `m` to toggle read. Categories, a phone reading view, and one-key clipping into Obsidian.

**WhisperSync** — An audiobook player that transcribes with Whisper and keeps the transcript scrolling in sync with the audio. Search across segments, bookmarks, speed control.

**Avian Visitors Companion** — Records the backyard, runs the clips through BirdNET, and keeps a dashboard of what's been singing. Thirty-seven species so far.

**Comic Generator** — Turns a text file into a three-panel comic strip. A cartoon universe is a JSON pack plus a folder of reference art, not code.

**Murphy Makes Math** — Printable first-grade math worksheets built on the things my son already likes: comics, stories, and drawing.

**Tour de Sacs** — A walking and biking game that turns the cul-de-sacs in my neighborhood into collectibles. Blocked mostly by Atlanta summer heat.

**LEGO Library** — A photo catalog of finished builds, one slideshow per set.

**Library of Everything** — A Jellyfin media library that generates a note for every show and album it takes in.

**Home Lab** — The Docker and Caddy stack most of the unlinked projects above actually run on.

**Machine Sync** — Syncthing keeping project folders in step across machines. Peer to peer, no cloud, no admin rights required.
