Things I've been building with AI agents, mostly on nights and weekends. Some are out
in the world. Some run on a server in my house. The rest are still being poked at.

---

## Shipped

**An Economic Robotic Rabbit Hole** — Tyler Cowen's conversation with Daron Acemoglu, annotated. The highlighted passages open the eighteen places I stopped to learn more. [Read it](/research/)

**Declaration of Innovation** — An open, remixable community charter for groups that gather to make, and make things better. [Visit website](https://declarationofinnovation.org/) · [Source code](https://github.com/agautsc/declaration-of-innovation)

**Mond(ai)y Coffee** — A free weekly coffee for AI builders and curious minds in Atlanta, Monday mornings at ATDC. The site carries the schedule, the speakers and the notes. [Visit website](https://mondaiycoffee.com/) · [Source code](https://github.com/Mond-AI-y-Coffee/website-main)

**Weird Al Stylebook** — A mostly serious guide to writing good and stuff, in the spirit of "Word Crimes," with a page that lets Weird Al check your grammar. [Visit website](https://agautsc.github.io/weird-al-stylebook/) · [Source code](https://github.com/agautsc/weird-al-stylebook)

**ARG at the ATDC** — Where to find me at work: the Monday coffee, the full ATDC calendar, and a link to book a one-on-one. [Visit website](https://argattheatdc.com/) · [Source code](https://github.com/agautsc/argattheatdc)

**gautsch.org** — This site: essays, a research notebook, and a decade of short posts brought over from LinkedIn. [Source code](https://github.com/agautsc/gautsch.org)

## Running on a server in my house

These aren't reachable from the open internet, so there's nothing to click.

**Google Reader 2026** — The RSS reader I missed. Add a feed, press `j` and `k` to move through it, `m` to toggle read. Categories, a phone reading view, and one-key clipping into Obsidian.

**WhisperSync** — An audiobook player that transcribes with Whisper and keeps the transcript scrolling in sync with the audio. Search across segments, bookmarks, speed control.

**Avian Visitors Companion** — Records the backyard, runs the clips through BirdNET, and keeps a dashboard of what's been singing, with a page for every bird.

**Public Art Map** — A mobile photo feed and map of Atlanta's public art, seeded from Wikimedia and OpenStreetMap. It rebuilds a 2015 experiment that mapped Instagram's #publicart posts until that API went away. [2015 source code](https://github.com/agautsc/public-art)

**LEGO Library** — A photo catalog of finished builds, one slideshow per set.

**Library of Everything** — A Jellyfin media library that generates a note for every show and album it takes in.

**Home Lab** — The Docker and Caddy stack the projects above run on.

**Machine Sync** — Syncthing keeping project folders in step across machines. Peer to peer, no cloud.

## Still being poked at

**Ask The Aux** — A shared-device party game. The group answers one prompt with songs and ends up with a playlist and a round recap. No hosted version yet. [Source code](https://github.com/agautsc/ask-the-aux)

**Comic Generator** — Turns a text file into a three-panel comic strip. A cartoon universe is a JSON pack plus a folder of reference art, not code.

**Murphy Makes Math** — Printable first-grade math worksheets built on the things my son already likes: comics, stories, and drawing.

**Folding Surprises** — Murphy's fold-in drawings, animated from photos of the paper so every line stays his marker. First up: the Toilet Monster. [See them](/folding-surprises/)

**Dog Hog** — My son's own comic about a scientist dog who rides a motorcycle. The AI handles the scans, layout and cover; the pencil lines stay his.

**Tour de Sacs** — A walking and biking game that turns the cul-de-sacs in my neighborhood into collectibles.

**Hike and Seek** — Printable bingo cards for family hikes, built from the real plants, landmarks and features of one trail. First up: Cloudland Canyon.
