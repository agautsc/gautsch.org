I suggest a new strategy, let the AI win.

One of the notes from Alex Lugo's demo yesterday was FilmPro's strategy to, for lack of a better phrase, let the AI win when it comes to development. Alex Lugo and Adolfo Rangel are both developers who can build a lot of things themselves. However, they try and focus on the framework of the tool as much as they can and expect the model development continue at speed to help fill in the features. It's part strategy and part necessity, but I think it's a different frame from old school development.
