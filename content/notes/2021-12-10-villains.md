Villains!

Since moving into our new house we've gotten postcards or mailers saying: * Our mortgage was "closed". This was an attempt to sell us insurance of some sort or another. * We missed a delivery. This was an attempt to sell us ADT. * And countless warranties and shields and such other words that are meant to seem both legit and necessary.

I'm always defending sales and marketing, but I can understand when companies like this are the primary experience of consumers why they hate the whole damn industry.
