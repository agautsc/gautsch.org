"We don’t send Claude to sell Claude, yet. We send people to sell Claude, even though Claude could probably generate the text to do the sales motion. People want to do commerce with other people, so I think that there’ll be certain relationships which get mediated by people, and people will have a strong preference, probably, for deals that they make on behalf of their larger pools of capital, where the deals are done by human proxies for large automated organizations or pools of capital." Jack Clark of Anthropic on Tyler Cowen's podcast.
