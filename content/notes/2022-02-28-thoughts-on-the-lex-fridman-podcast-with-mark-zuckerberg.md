Thoughts on the Lex Fridman podcast with Mark Zuckerberg https://lnkd.in/ewS7-gSe

\1. Zuckerberg still has the fight in him.  You listen to his contemporaries from a generation before (Gates, Bezos etc) they're now focused on forming their legacy for future generations. Zuckerberg is still very much focused on running his company and trying to promote and defend it against current day threats.

\2. I'd get more from Zuckerberg talking about surfing for an hour than hearing him defend his company's bad reputation. That stuff is all too practiced to be interesting.

\3. The story about talking to the Rabbi about Genesis gave a fun twist to what could have been pretty standard to answer to, "what is the meaning of life." To be said another way, I enjoyed that answer.
