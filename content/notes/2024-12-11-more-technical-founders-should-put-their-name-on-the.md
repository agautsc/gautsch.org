More technical founders should put their name on the company.

That's one thing that went through my head when reading "The Birth of Loud: Leo Fend, Les Paul, and the Guitar-Pioneering Rivalry That Shaped Rock 'N' Roll."

Leo Fender was the founder. The company grew and became the genre defining business it did because of a team that included talented and important sales and operations people. At the end of the day, the guitars hit a standard of quality though, in part, because Leo's last name was on the guitar.
