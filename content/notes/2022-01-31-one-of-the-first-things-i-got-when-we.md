One of the first things I got when we all started working from was https://lnkd.in/eCytSDi mouse sharing software. It lets me share a mouse and keyboard between two computers.

Like a home printer, it worked great when it worked, but if I didn't use it for a while, re-connecting and setting up things got wonky.

The last wonky setup sent me searching for a new alternative. Welp, if you live in only MSFT world may I suggest Mouse without Borders. https://lnkd.in/eef7WmX5 Setup was much smoother and has worked great so far.
