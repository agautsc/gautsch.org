How do I make those "A Fly On The Call clips (https://lnkd.in/eTPNcPS)"? A question one person asked, this was my answer:

\1. Edit the audio to clip you want to use 2. Upload to https://sonix.ai/ to get a transcript of the audio. 3. Export the transcript as an SRT file. SRT files are the files used for Closed Captioning or, in this case, open caption. 4. In Photoshop create two images that are transparent PNG's one with my picture and the talk bubble and one with Monique Mills, MBA, PMP®. 5. Create a Premiere Pro project with the orange color matte and A Fly On The Call clip at the bottom. 6. Import the SRT file. You can then customize where the text is, how big the text is, and what it looks like. (I spent too long trying to have the text in different locations depending on who was speaking. That was DUMB. 7. Import the images of the speaker and place them on the timeline depending on who's talking.

This did take longer than I expected the first time, but the process moved much quicker the second time.

I'm also teaching myself all this stuff with YouTube videos and guesswork. Any suggestions for an improved process are welcomed.
