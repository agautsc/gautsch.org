If being a better note taker or journaler or writer is a 2021 goal then give Obsidian https://obsidian.md/ a shot.

It works for me as a tool. Mapping random thoughts and divergent paths in a single place.

It also works for me as breath of old-air in terms of a business model. * You can download the software with a free and SHOCKING one-time only fees for purchasing the software. * No login required. * You host the files where you want.

It's so 20th century in it's business model. I hope they succeed.
