We got a bird feeder with a camera for Christmas. For most of January it was more a squirrel feeder than a bird feeder. We went through a big bag of seed and got tons activity on the camera. Mostly squirrels and cardinals. Our second bag was spicy seeds. Squirrels apparently hate spicy stuff and it doesn't bother birds.

Our activity dropped greatly, but look at this cool Downey woodpecker who came for some food and a photo.

Could be a parable for marketing spend or maybe just a chance to share a bird pic on LinkedIn. You decide.
