We've been meeting every Monday for a couple months to talk AI. At the start, the group was small and the topics varied.

As the group started to grow, a single conversation about free will, self-driving cars, and the future of humanity got harder to managed. So, we started to narrow focus. Now, we're interested to learn from those with dirty hands, building the future.

This week, Alex Lugo shared FilmPro. Not just what FilmPro is, but HOW he (and Adolfo Rangel) built FilmPro. The goal, show people what can be built with small, passionate teams and a lot of AI know how.
