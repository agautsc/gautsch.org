I'm not a graphic designer. I am interested in how AI tools work in that space. I do need to promote our sales and marketing roundtables. I hate when things looks like they were designed in Canva. I am excited about the Oppenheimer movie.

And here we are.

A couple notes on how I used AI to put this thing together.

\1. I thought I would be able to use the Photoshop AI to expand the background of a vertical poster to make the explosion fit a horizontal layout. That looked terrible. I ended up just searching for a horizontal Oppenheimer poster. 2. Photoshop AI did a great job of removing the text. I just selected the section of the image with the text and told the AI to remove the text and it did it perfectly. 3. I ended up using Bing Chat to ask what font is used in the Oppenheimer movie poster and what font Adobe Fonts has that are similar to Gotham. This worked pretty amazingly. I had to use Bing over ChatGPT because Bing uses the internet and ChatGPT doesn't. There certainly are other ways to find answers to those questions using the traditional internet, but I found that the Bing Chat interface to be easier and quicker than any traditional process.

As you can tell, no graphic designer would be worried about their job from my work, but it was fun to put this together quickly this morning.
