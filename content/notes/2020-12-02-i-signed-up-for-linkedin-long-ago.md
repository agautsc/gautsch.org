I signed up for LinkedIn long ago. Maybe it was a competitor to Plaxo I was testing out. (Remember Plaxo?) Maybe it was the next thing after it. I don't exactly remember.

I do know I gave LinkedIn little thought since then and the few thoughts I gave it were thoughts of disdain or snobbery. Not my sandbox.

But things change. I talk to Monique Mills, MBA, PMP®every week (http://aflyonthecall.com/) and she groks the value of LinkedIn. I've started to listen to her.

I also figure I'm putting effort into things like A Fly On The Call (http://aflyonthecall.com/) and classes and events for the ATDC and I want folks to show up. I'm not interested in putting in the effort to just rake the sand smooth again without anyone knowing about it.

And so, I figured this next thing is worth sharing on LinkedIn. Maybe he'll inspire me to share more too.

Casey Graham is sharing the story of how Gravy broke the LinkedIn algorithm. If you and your company could do better with sales or recruiting and don't have a huge budget then this might make sense for you. https://lnkd.in/ercBgyS

If it does, sign on up.
