The first piece of software I bought when the WFH world began was Synergy. https://lnkd.in/eCytSDi

It allows you to use one keyboard and mouse for multiple computers. For example, I can have my Surface in tablet mode and my laptop open and use the mouse and keyboard from the laptop for both computers. I can even copy and paste across computers.

It's a pretty amazing piece of tech. It works across platforms, so you can have Windows laptop and an iPad up and running at the same time.

I'd be lying if I said I used it all of the time. Sometimes you just don't need that many screens. But, for example, when I was putting together that little whiteboard video from yesterday, it was nice to have Synergy running. #techtiptuesday #productivity #productivitysoftware
