I was working on something. I wanted to reference a person I follow on Instagram. I opened the app and clicked to search to find this person.

Five minutes later I look back at my computer screen having just watched several short standup clips. I had forgotten to look for the profile that inspired me to open the app in the first place.

This is no way to live or work.
