Gamers created the AI revolution

When you work on the non-hard tech side of the startup world it's amazing how often folks dismiss the work you do as trivial or unimportant. Often times right to your face.

Listening to Tim Sweeney on the Lex Fridman podcast helps to underline something that is obvious. The unimportant task of playing video games gave us companies who cared deeply and worked hard to build the GPUs and optimize the code that is creating our future.

Take seriously whatever you're working on and make it great. It might just turn out to change the world.
