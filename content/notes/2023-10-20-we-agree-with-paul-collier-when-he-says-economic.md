We agree with Paul Collier when he says, “Economic growth is not a cure-all, but lack of growth is a kill-all.”

Or, as Bob would tell his ma, "that he not busy being born is busy dying."

Or, as Bobby McCormick would tell us, "Don’t worry so much about being done. Few things really great are ever finished."

I don't know if it is the most important line in Marc's manifesto, but it is sure hits on something I think to be important. It's easy to get frustrated by technology or chuckle at late-stage capitalism. But we all need to continue forward and upward. Stagnation is death and death is bad.
