Coming 2006!

I ran across this screenshot this morning. It's one of, if not the first, websites OrangeCoat created. On my first day selling, I emailed the President of Clemson and the President of IPTAY, completely destroying their websites. They both responded, and one of them even bought a website!

We built a website selling the dream of the West End Zone. Twenty-one years later, I was hanging out in the shadow of the West End Zone with my son on my shoulders, watching the Savannah Bananas put on a show.

I wish I could tell you I thought back to the work we did to help get the West End Zone built that day. I didn't. Memories of past clients rarely cross my mind. It's nice to see the screenshot today and make the connection.

Business is a constant forward march. Celebrate your wins when you can and take some pictures for future you.
