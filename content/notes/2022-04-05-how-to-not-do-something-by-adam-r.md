How to not do something by Adam R. Gautsch:

Step 1: I should try to sketch a little every day. Step 2: Maybe something from the garden. Step 3: Maybe the same plant every day. Step 4: The same tomato plant in a pot each day and from the same angle and the same time of day. I could then "see" the plant grow over time. Step 5: I should draw the same tomato plant from the same angle every day so I can see it grow and also take notes about the growth each day to learn what is changing and be a better tomato grower. Step 6: Maybe start Instagram account about this? Step 7: What domain names are available?

Just draw the damn tomato!
