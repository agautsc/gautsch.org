My general opinion of <a href="https://www.tomsachs.com/" title="Cool signature though">Tom Sachs</a> has always been that I enjoy his project management skills more than his finished art. I can't imagine seeing any of his exhibitions and being moved. <a href="https://youtu.be/49p1JVLHUos?si=EyZ1qm-RRztd_Tc3" title="If I understand things correctly. The idea of Ten Bullets is from one his employees and not even him. Goes to management skills.">Ten Bullets</a> is cool video to teach new employees the philosophy and ethic of a shop though. 

I was listening to a talk of his today and he used the phrase "a genuine fake" to describe his recreation of Broadway Boogie Woogie using plywood and gaffer tape and something clicked. It's his art not mine. From photography to photocopiers to AI exact recreations and perfect beautiful art is too easy. Dentist office oil paintings are going to be even easier to make. 

Art is going to become more personal.
