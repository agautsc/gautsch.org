  • How they get made

    1. An idea goes on the board. Each one starts as a note on our family idea board, with questions for Murphy.
    2. The robots build a first playable. Small and quick, to find out if it's fun. Bro! was playable the day it was filed.
    3. Murphy takes over the art. His drawings get scanned and traced into the game, not redrawn. He names things and draws new levels on paper.
    4. We play it. What we notice becomes the next round.
