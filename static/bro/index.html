BRO!

Slade captured the Titans and made Pac-Man copies of them. Pop the copies and set the real ones free!

    Match 3 copies and they go BRO! Pop the copies next to a cage to free a real Titan, who can beat any color. Every miss charges Slade. When he's full, he stomps the ceiling down.

    Drag to aim, let go to shoot. Tap NEXT to swap. Bounce shots off the walls. Characters and names by Murphy.

    LEVELS

    Make your own level: color in a level sheet and we'll type it in.

      Small letter = that Titan in a cage (r). X = Slade block. . = empty. Rows take turns: 8 spots, then 7.
